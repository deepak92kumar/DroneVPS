# 2 November 2026 — four flights, and what each one measured

Logs 113, 114 and 115 on the FC card; runs vps_00406 and vps_00411 on the
Jetson. Position is scored against `gps1_north_m` / `gps1_east_m` only.

## Log 113, 08:58 — the node died four times

`UnboundLocalError: local variable '_fe' referenced before assignment`, four
times, from a variable assigned only inside the `else` of the frame-age check
and read on every frame below it. systemd restarted in 5 s each time, but the
real outage is 8.4–37.2 s because a cold start also pays for the DepthAI
pipeline boot and a fresh flow baseline. GPS2 went stale and the aircraft sat
in ALT_HOLD at 30 m.

Fixed, and the whole class gated: see `bench/unbound_locals.py` and
`tests/test_node_does_not_die.py`. The same shape had already killed the node
once before as `_use_pp` (76 crashes). Two more live instances were found by
the new checker and fixed: `ts` in `LiveSource.read()` and `got` in
`gps_rc_switch.set_and_verify()`.

## Log 114 / run 406, 09:57 — it held, and the matcher did not

308 s unbroken on VPS at 71.6 m. Position drifted 4.6 → 85.3 m.

The matcher submitted 789 jobs, completed 304, and the fuser used ONE. Of 777
frames replayed through the production path, **zero** landed within 15 m of
the truth. Neither score nor margin separates anything: corr(score, error)
+0.148, corr(margin, error) −0.095.

The roll and pitch the pilot saw on the GPS descent is this drift being paid
back. At t 592.2 the EKF position moved 88.0 m in six seconds while the
aircraft's own speed was 0.41 m/s — the estimate snapped onto the M9N — and
LOITER then flew 139.7 m over 128 s to reach where it now believed it should
be. VIBE, motor spread, battery and the M9N were all clean in that window,
and attitude error was 3.49° rms, so the aircraft was following its commands;
the commands were large because the position error was.

## Run 411 / log 115, 13:23 — half the flow was thrown away

105 s on VPS at 70.6 m, same site as 406 nine hours earlier.

The estimate was **already 35.2 m out at the moment of the switch**. Over the
leg the error swung between 1.5 and 38.0 m. gps1 says the aircraft covered
211.7 m in 105 s and ended 40.6 m from where it started, inside a 54 × 57 m
box — the pilot's "kahi rukta nahi", measured.

The velocity handed to the FC was not the aircraft's: sent p50 3.96 m/s
against a true 2.15, p90 at the 6.00 m/s cap and saturated 16% of the leg,
and corr(sent, true) = **+0.006**. LOITER damps on velocity, so it corrected,
overshot and reversed.

One number explains it, against 406 at the same place and height:

| | 406 | 411 |
|---|---|---|
| `flow_disagree` p50 | 0.070 | **0.150** |
| `flow_disagree` p90 | 0.170 | 0.860 |
| over the 0.15 gate | 13% | **48%** |
| frames with no step | 14% | **50%** |
| actual flow rate | 20.3 Hz | **10.7 Hz** |
| flow-derived speed | 2.17 | 4.38 m/s |
| sent speed p50 | 1.77 | 3.96 m/s |

100% of frames over `flow_disagree_max_px` produced no step, in both runs, so
the gate behaves exactly as written. In 411 it threw away half the flow, and
with half the samples the N-frame baseline stopped averaging the per-step
noise down. Per-step size (0.21 → 0.25 m), step dt (0.0399 s in both) and
directional bias (0.062 → 0.052) are all but unchanged — this is the baseline
losing samples, not the flow getting worse per sample.

**Not established:** why the two cameras disagreed twice as much. Same place,
same altitude, 09:57 against 13:23, so light is the obvious suspect and only
a suspect. Nothing was changed on the strength of it.

## Maps

`bambattu 1.tif` is the pilot's own orthomosaic at 0.050 m/px and it is the
only sheet that has ever produced correct fixes here — 8 of 120 within 5 m,
2 within 2 m, where the 0.509 m/px Google sheet gave 0 of 532 within 15 m.
It had to be converted from Int16 (MapCache cannot open that) and
downsampled, because a match at native 5 cm costs 14.8 s against a 2.0 s
period; 0.30 m/px costs 0.63 s and scores best.

It covers only 282 × 169 m, which at 60 m leaves a usable box of 95 × 19 m.
`bhh.tif`, loaded later for the new site, is 3.31 × 2.12 km but is 0.509 m/px
satellite imagery — the kind that does not match.
