## 1. Where the system actually is

The VPS holds position below about 30 m and does not above it.

| altitude | what happens | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 9-18 m | holds, minutes at a time | log 228 block 4: **167 s, no failsafe**; block 3 of log 228: 104 s at 16.5 m, drifting 3.6 m with 15% stick |
| 28-31 m | holds | log 231: two windows at 27.7 and 28.5 m, no failsafe, one ran 74 s |
| 34-77 m | ALT_HOLD within a minute | log 231: three windows at 34.7, 56.8, 76.8 m, all failed |

The map, when fixes flow, reaches the number the pilot remembered from the
pre-SSD system:

| run | map fixes | fix rate | position error above 60 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| vps_00038 | 0 | 0.00/s | 39.69 m |
| vps_00047 | 0 | 0.00/s | 38.40 m |
| vps_00054 | 5 | 0.05/s | 38.07 m |
| **vps_00066** | **19** | **0.27/s** | **4.50 m** |
| vps_00076 | 98 | 0.40/s | 9.77 m |
| vps_00091 | 2 | 0.04/s | 8.51 m |

**The system error is set by the fix RATE, not by the accuracy of any one
fix.** A single fix is 18-22 m and that error is random rather than biased -
mean offset 15.1 m against a spread of 19.9 m - so averaging N of them falls
as 1/sqrt(N) and about 25 reach 4 m. Do not confuse the per-fix number with
the system number; I did, for most of a day.

## 3. The two mistakes I made, and what they teach

**Unequal smoothing.** I claimed the accel blend doubled the reported speed
and changed `tau_s` on it. The flow-only figure was smoothed over 30 samples
and the module's output was read raw. Compared identically the blend HALVES
the flow velocity. Reverted before flight.

**Scoring a hold by XKF4.SV.** On 22 Oct I shipped two changes that improved
SV and made the aircraft hold twelve times worse - 0.9 m over 23 s became
11.1 m over 24 s at 31 m - because SV is the EKF agreeing with ITSELF, and
anything that makes our output quieter flatters it by construction. In the
17 m window our speed read p50 0.12 m/s with 77% deadbanded while the aircraft
moved at 0.65. **The metric for a hold is how far the aircraft ran from the
switch point, against the raw receiver, in m/s. Report that first.** Ask of
every change: does this make us quieter? If so, be suspicious.

Both are in `memory/dronevps-measure-fairly.md` with the earlier scalar-metric
mistake.

## 4. DO NOT RETRY. Each of these was measured; most were flown.

### Height-scaled gains - three flown, three reverted

The founding brief says *"never scale with height... scaling them previously
cost two flights and was reverted."* The two constants it names
(`corr_rate_mps`, `reanchor_m`) are dead code now, but the rule was never
about them.

* `flow_vel_frames_high` 60 above 25 m. **Flown as log 229, reverted the same
  day.** At 31.6 m the aircraft ran 11.1 m in 24 s against 0.9 m in 23.2 s at
  31.5 m on log 228. A longer baseline reports less of the real motion.
  The ramp machinery is still there, set flat, so a retry needs a flight and
  not a rewrite.
* `flow_noise_rate` 0.25 -> 0.9. **Flown as log 229, reverted.** The fit was
  honest - error against the raw receiver really is 0.897*sqrt(t) over ten
  blocks - and the hold got worse anyway. **This aircraft has no other
  horizontal position source.** An EKF told its only source is poor does not
  find a better one; it weights the estimate down and the position controller
  is handed a sloppier position. What is declared now is knowingly optimistic.

### The map's fix rate - both knobs flown and reverted

* `max_correction_m` 0.5 -> 1.5-3.0, 3 Oct: *"the hold fell from 11-14 s to
  4.5-9.0 s while map_move_m sat at exactly 1.50."* Reverted that afternoon.
  A saturated cap means the matcher is persistently far from the estimate, so
  raising it hands the estimate to whichever is wrong.
* `fix_period` 2.0 -> 1.0, tried 22 Oct from the archived 11 Oct state.
  **Flown as log 227 and reverted:** at 82-83 m XKF4.SP went 0.13 (log 226,
  2.0 s) to 1.17 (log 227, 1.0 s) against a 1.2 gate. A fix moves the POSITION
  and not the velocity, so at 1 Hz the EKF is told twice as often that the
  aircraft jumped without moving. **BUT SEE SECTION 6** - that test was
  confounded and this one is still open.

### The matcher - nine things tested, one adopted

| tried | result |
|---|---|
| window `0.60*alt` -> `0.90*alt` | accept 27.8% -> 11.1%; +20 m more -> 2.8%. More map area gives XFeat more chances to match WRONG while the query covers the same ground |
| map upscaled 2x | raw matches 124 -> 241, inliers 8 -> **1**. Upscaling adds interpolation, not detail |
| fitting rotation+scale instead of pure translation | inliers 6 -> 16, accept 40% -> 68%, position **unmoved**: 21.8 -> 20.9 m, and 18.8 -> 18.6 on frames where both pass. The extra inliers are RANSAC's extra freedom |
| NCC refinement after XFeat | 22.3 -> 21.0 m, two thirds better and one third worse, 0.0019 s. **Promising but n=15** and the correlation peak cleared 0.5 on one fix only. Worth more data, not shipping |
| undistorting the frame | inliers 6 -> 6, error 20.7 -> 20.3 m. The distortion is real and large (p50 4.2 px, 52.7 px at the corners) but `north_up_query` keeps the CENTRE, so at 85 m a 160x160 query comes from a 243x169 rescaled frame - about the middle 66% - and the corners never enter it |
| a sharper or higher-resolution map | on the SAME ground at the same pixel size **the map is sharper than our query**, 3.15 vs 2.23 by a 2x downscale test. The query is the soft one, from the 4.7x downscale |
| a second camera, or averaging frames | consecutive frames' fixes agree to 2.6 m while both are 19.3 m wrong; averaging three made it 5% worse. The error is the SCENE. Two views 7.5 cm apart give 0.70 px of parallax at 85 m and would agree with each other just as closely |
| a separate CPU core for the matcher | XFeat takes 0.15-0.20 s against a 2.0 s budget, already runs in its own thread, OpenCV and torch release the GIL |
| lowering `min_inliers` | nothing. At the ~124 matches this matcher returns, `min_inliers` 12 and `min_score` 0.10 are the SAME constraint |
| **`min_score` 0.10 -> 0.07** | **adopted.** accept 34.6% -> 42.3% at 75-90 m, per-fix error unchanged |

### Hardware questions, settled by measurement

* **Mono resolution.** `THE_800_P` is this sensor's MAXIMUM, not a choice.
  `getConnectedCameraFeatures` reports CAM_B and CAM_C as OV9282 with exactly
  640x400, 1280x720, 1280x800. `THE_1200_P` is in depthai's enum for other
  sensors and crashes the Myriad X with `RTEMS_FATAL_SOURCE_ASSERT` 4.4 s
  after boot in every configuration, even one stream with no depth and no IMU.
  The pilot's "1200" means 1280x800, which is what runs.
* **The RGB sensor is the only resolution headroom.** CAM_A is an IMX378
  offering up to 4056x3040, with fx about 1943 at 2560x1440 against the mono's
  791.72 - 2.45x the pixels on the same ground. **It is not in the pipeline.**
  Untested for the map. This is the one unexplored lever on the camera side.
* **Stereo depth cannot be a height.** It reads a constant 5.40 m above 60 m -
  min and max both 5.40 in two runs - and 4.24 m at a true 14 m. The 7.5 cm
  baseline is good for a few metres. `_depth_gate` correctly stands itself
  down above `DEPTH_MAX_M` 30.
* **The compass in use is the M9N module's**, and it is innocent of the
  "GPS Glitch or Compass error" message. `COMPASS_PRIO1_ID` 658953 decodes as
  devtype 0x0A = IST8310 on I2C bus 1 address 0x0E with `COMPASS_EXTERNAL` 1,
  and the log says "EKF3 IMU1 MAG0 ... complete". PRIO2 is an RM3100 on SPI,
  internal, kept as a fallback. `XKF4.SM` over all of log 231 is p50 0.010,
  max 0.060 against a 1.2 gate. ArduPilot emits one message for two causes.

## 5. Hardware state the next session needs to know

* **The rangefinder is DISABLED** (`RNGFND1_TYPE` 0), by the pilot, and that
  is right. Its zero wanders 1-2 m over minutes and reached 6.37 m sitting on
  the ground, while ArduPilot's land detector needs the rangefinder unhealthy
  or under 2 m - so `LDET.Count` stayed 0 for a whole log and the aircraft
  would not disarm. The sensor MEASURES fine (`lidar = 0.981*alt + 8.51`,
  corr 0.989); only its zero moves. `EK3_RNG_USE_HGT` is -1 and
  `EK3_SRC1_POSZ` is 1, so nothing needed it. With it off, disarm works.
* **The battery current sensor reads 0.00 A.** `BATT_CURR_PIN` is now 17,
  which CUAV and ArduPilot both document for the X7's analog input, but the
  Power A current line may simply not be wired. **The test is under load:** if
  motors spinning give real amps the wire is there; if it stays 0.00 it is
  not. Voltage (pin 16) is correct and the pilot confirmed 24.8 V with a
  checker. Every mAh figure is fiction until this is settled, and `BATT_FS_*`
  failsafe ACTIONS are both 0 - thresholds set, no action taken.
* **`ARMING_SKIPCHK` is 6156** and nothing in our code writes it.
* **Do not probe the FC with extra MAVLink clients.** `mavlink_router` owns the
  one serial line, so every client shares one channel and one set of stream
  rates. My probes stopped EXTRA3 and the node's `rngfnd_m` went empty for
  three runs; it could not be restored from another client, and the node asks
  for its streams once at startup. Read the node's CSV instead - it already
  carries everything. See `memory/dronevps-dont-probe-the-fc-live.md`.
* The camera leaves crash dumps in `.cache/depthai/crashdumps/` on teardown
  and after a 1200p attempt. It recovers; the dumps are not a fault in flight.

## 6. Open, in the order I would take them

1. **`fix_period` 2.0 -> 1.0. The pilot's call, and it is the live one.**
   Their archived 11 Oct state was 1 fix/s with a 0.5 m cap, which is what
   produced the 4-6 m they remember, and they have said so twice. My evidence
   against it - log 227's SP 9x worse - came from a flight that was also
   sending 25.47 m/s to the FC. **That test was confounded and the velocity is
   now capped at 6.0.** Fix rate is the only thing measured to move the map's
   contribution. SP is the thing to watch.
2. **Verify the cap at 30-60 m.** `max_sent_mps` 6.0 plus the capped-sample
   accuracy went in after log 231 and have not flown. Below 30 m the cap fired
   on 0.0% of samples, so nothing there should change; above 40 m it saturates
   7.8-31% of the time and that is what the new accuracy is for.
3. **A heading-diverse flight above 70 m.** Accepted fixes show a mean offset
   of N +2.1 +/- 16.8 and **E -15.0 +/- 10.5 m** - on east the mean exceeds
   the spread, which hints at a real offset - but all 21 accepted samples sit
   in one heading band, so a fixed offset cannot be separated from a
   heading-dependent one. Costs one flight, no code. The pilot has already
   tested a bias constant and it did not help, so do not add one blind.
4. **Fly to 110 m.** The reports put the matcher's own error at 17.81 m at
   95-110 m and 68.36 m at 60-70 m, and 4.13 m of system error at 110 m. Our
   ceiling so far is 95.8 m, with 14 frames in that band.
5. **The RGB camera for the map.** 2.45x the pixels on the same ground, and
   our query is measurably the softer half of the match. Needs a bench test
   for VPU and USB headroom first - 1200p on the monos crashed the firmware,
   so do not assume.
6. **The flow's velocity above 45 m.** Flow age p99 crosses the 0.6 s timeout
   there (0.613 at 45-65 m, 0.775 at 65-90 m, against 0.134 below 25 m) and
   dead reckoning is gated on `v.valid`, so once flow has been down that long
   **the position stops being updated at all** - 118 freezes on log 229, 20.5 s
   total, max 0.47 s, up to 2.1 m unobserved. Below 25 m it costs 2.3 s and
   0.0 m, so it is an altitude fault. Not changed.
