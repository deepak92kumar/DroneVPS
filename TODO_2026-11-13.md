## 1 — Ready to apply, measured, NOT flown

### `VEL_ACC_PER_M` 0.048 -> 0.025

The declared speed accuracy is about twice the real error. Measured over
37600 samples from the four flights of 11-13 November, sent velocity
against the M9N on a 1 s baseline:

| alt band | real p68 (1 sigma) | declared at 0.048 | % above it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-20 m | 0.37 | 0.92 | 2% |
| 20-40 m | 0.96 | 2.06 | 8% |
| 40-60 m | 1.60 | 2.98 | 11% |
| 60-80 m | 2.38 | 4.34 | 12% |
| 80-100 m | 2.36 | 4.45 | 5% |
| 100-120 m | 2.72 | 5.88 | 3% |

A one-sigma figure should be exceeded about a third of the time. At 0.048
it is exceeded 3-12%. Best fit through the measured p68 is
`0.29 + 0.0247 x alt`; 0.025 with the 0.6 floor lands at 22-31% exceeded.

**What it buys:** the "GPS Glitch or Compass error" crossing moves from
about 21 m to about 40 m, so the aircraft is quiet lower down. On 13 Nov
the glitch fired at 28.6 m and cleared at 7.6 m, which puts the EKF's
tolerance between 0.96 and 1.63 m/s.

**What it does not buy:** at a 110 m mission both formulas are far above
that tolerance, so the glitch stays during the mission. Removing it
entirely would mean declaring 1.3 m/s at 110 m where the truth is 2.72 -
the kind of lie that cost us on 20 October.

**Risk:** the EKF would trust this velocity more, so a bad sample carries
further. `EK3_SRC1_VELXY` is 0 so it is never fused, but the glitch check
and the innovation gates read it. That path has never flown at 0.025.

**How:** one line in `live_node.py`, restart the node, fly it ALONE
against the 13 November baseline above. It was set on 13 Nov and reverted
the same day before flying, for the demo.

## 2 — Measured and REJECTED, do not retry without new evidence

### A speed ceiling in `gate_step`

The pixel ceiling opens with altitude - 50 px is 6.9 m per frame at 110 m,
166 m/s at 24 Hz - so a `min()` against 12 m/s went in. Replayed over three
flights before flying it:

| flight | clipped | dead-reckoned drift p50, old -> new |
|---|---|---|
| 531 | 25.5% | 28.22 -> 69.75 m |
| 526 | 27.4% | 24.82 -> 56.82 m |
| 521 | 26.0% | 45.98 -> 81.73 m |

Two to three times worse, every flight. A step is real motion PLUS noise,
so the largest steps are the ones where the aircraft was moving; clipping
them throws away real displacement and the estimate lags. The pixel ceiling
is loose at altitude but loose SYMMETRICALLY; a speed cap is not.

### A wider-FOV camera

Velocity error is `pixels x altitude / focal`, so a shorter focal makes the
dominant error WORSE. At 77.6 deg and fx 795 one pixel is 0.138 m at 110 m;
at 120 deg it would be 0.298 m - the velocity noise doubles. It would help
rotation tolerance (1 deg would be 6.4 px instead of 14) and give a 381 m
footprint instead of 177 m, but the thing limiting us is the velocity noise.

### The 4K colour camera for flow

It is ROLLING shutter (IMX378); the mono pair (OV9282) is global. Readout is
about 25 ms, so across one frame the aircraft rotates 0.6 deg in calm flight
and 3.6 deg in a turn - 25 to 150 px of shear against a flow signal of
1-7 px per frame. The shear also differs by image row, so derotation cannot
remove it.

**What would help, if hardware is ever bought:** more pixels at the SAME
FOV and global shutter. AR0234 at 1920x1200 gives 0.092 m/px at 110 m
against the present 0.138 - about 1.5x, not 3x.
