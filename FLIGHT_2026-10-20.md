## 1. Disarm kyon nahi hua — root cause mil gaya

**Lidar ka absolute reading 2 m se upar tha, aur ArduPilot ka land detector
2 m se niche ka rangefinder maangta hai.**

ArduPilot me landed declare hone ke liye ye *sab* ek saath chahiye:

```
motor_at_lower_limit && throttle_mix_at_min && !large_angle_request
&& !large_angle_error && accel_stationary && descent_rate_low
&& rangefinder_check && WoW      (rangefinder_check = lidar kharab ho YA < 2 m padhe)
```

Log 216 ke `LDET` me `Count=0` **pure log me, ek baar bhi nahi badha** — matlab
condition kabhi true nahi hui. Landing ke baad (135.8–149.2 s) flags `1744` par
atke rahe = THROTTLE_MIX_AT_MIN + MOTOR_AT_LOWER_LIMIT + ACCEL_STATIONARY +
DESCENT_RATE_LOW + WOW — yani **sirf rangefinder_check missing**. Lidar us waqt
2.30 m padh raha tha, zameen par khadi machine par.

Confirmation: 155.6 / 156.6 / 157.7 s par `RANGEFINDER_BELOW_2M` bit on hota hai
— theek un teen moments par jab reading 1.8–1.9 m tak dipp ki. Pure 65 s ke log
me yeh bit sirf wahi 3 baar hai.

### Doosra blocker (jo pehle se khud ban gaya)

Landing ke baad LOITER ka position controller band nahi hua (kyunki landed
detect nahi hua), aur I-term wind up ho gaya:

| t (s) | target accel N/E | DesRoll / DesPitch | motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 137 | −0.24 / +0.51 | −1.2° / −2.9° | 1150–1250 |
| 149 | −1.29 / +2.29 | −5.4° / −13.9° | 1150–1250 |
| 150+ | −1.0 / +2.4 | −7° / −13° | **M1 1491, M3 1390, M4 1324, M6 1491** |

Position error sirf 0.09 m north / 0.40 m east tha — 13.9° ka lean us error se
nahi, integrator se aaya (machine zameen par thi, hil nahi sakti thi, I-term
chadhta gaya). 149 s par `LARGE_ANGLE_ERROR` + `LARGE_ANGLE_REQUEST` dono set ho
gaye, jo land detector ko *independently* block karte hain. Aur motors idle
(1150) se 1491 tak chale gaye — machine 23 second tak zameen ko dhakka de rahi
thi. **Props ke saath yeh tip-over/flip ka risk hai.**

### Isliye "bina prop arm/disarm ho jata hai, throttle dene ke baad nahi hota"

`land_complete` boot/arm par pehle se **true** hota hai aur takeoff par clear
hota hai. Takeoff hi nahi kiya (log 215) → flag true raha → DISARM_DELAY=2 ne
kaam kar diya. Ek baar udne ke baad usko *dobara kamana* padta hai, aur lidar ne
wo rasta band kar diya. Yeh mode-independent hai — STABILIZE/ALT_HOLD me bhi
same block lagega.

## 2. Lidar: relative bilkul theek, absolute drift kar raha hai

| | log 215 (ground) | log 216 (ground) | log 216 (hover) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RFND.Dist` | **0.82 m** | **2.53 m** | 7.00 m |
| board `Vcc` | 5.088 ± 0.008 | 5.101 ± 0.015 | 5.089 ± 0.012 |

Teen minute ke andar, usi zameen par, 0.82 → 2.53 m (ADC par +23 mV). Vcc
stable hai, to 5 V rail wajah nahi hai (`RNGFND1_RMETRIC=1` current-loop sensor
ke liye galat setting hai, par uska asar sirf ~0.02 m — concept galat hai,
aaj ka culprit nahi).

**Relative accuracy excellent:** hover 7.00 − ground 2.53 = **4.47 m**, EKF ka
Alt 4.45 m. 2 cm ka farak. Isliye altitude estimate flight me theek tha — EKF
rangefinder ko takeoff reference ke relative use karta hai, constant bias cancel
ho jata hai. Jo cheez cancel nahi hoti wo hai land detector ka absolute 2 m test.

Wajah structural hai: 4–20 mA loop, 150 Ω resistor, 180 m full scale → 1 mV ≈
7 cm. Yani ±1–2 m absolute error is chain se *expected* hai. 19 Oct ka
single-point cal (0.26 m par) tik nahi sakta. `TF03_4-20mA_CUAV_X7_Wiring_Guide.md`
(repo me hai, HANDOFF me nahi) khud kehti hai is model ka UART sirf config ke
liye hai, data nahi deta — to digital par switch karna option nahi hai.

## 3. Vibration — FC: theek. Camera: resonance nahi, par do asli bug.

### FC (dataflash `VIBE`, hover 108–121 s)

| | VibeX | VibeY | VibeZ | clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IMU0 | 0.80 | 1.58 | **2.28** (max 3.64) | 0 |
| IMU1 | 0.56 | 0.83 | **2.21** (max 3.55) | 0 |

Zero clipping, VibeZ ~2.3 m/s². Yeh accha hai (30 failsafe limit, 15 se niche
healthy, 5 se niche ideal). Frame mechanically theek hai.

### Spectrum (batch IMU `ISBH/ISBD`, 2013 Hz, hover)

| axis | rms >12 Hz | peaks |
|---|---|---|
| accel X | 0.85 m/s² | **47** · 55 · 102 · 112 · 204 Hz |
| accel Y | 1.69 m/s² | 26 · **49** · 57 · 104 · **112** Hz |
| accel Z | 2.42 m/s² | **47** · 55 · 104 · 293 · 307 · 334 Hz |
| gyro X/Y/Z | 0.10–0.11 rad/s | **49** · 57 · 102–114 Hz |

- Fundamental (shaft) **47–49 Hz** = ~2850 RPM. Blade-pass 102–112 Hz.
- **Do alag RPM group hain: 47 Hz aur 57 Hz** — motors ke beech ~20% RPM spread.
  Yeh motor PWM spread (niche §4) se match karta hai.
- **Harmonic notch galat jagah hai.** `INS_HNTCH_MODE=1` (throttle), `FREQ=127`,
  `REF=0.24`, `BW=60`. Hover throttle 0.331 par notch = 127·√(0.331/0.24) =
  **~149 Hz**. Asli peak 47 Hz par hai. Notch BW ke saath 119–179 Hz cover kar
  raha hai — 47 Hz ko chhu bhi nahi raha. Effectively notch off hai.

### Camera side

Raw camera-IMU vibration log nahi hoti, par per-frame derotation se pata chalta
hai: hover me camera gyro **0.41 °/frame rms** = 12.3 °/s, aur FC ka khud ka
measured roll rate std **12.6 °/s**. Dono same magnitude → **camera mount
resonate nahi kar raha**, camera frame ke saath hi hil raha hai. Camera-specific
vibration ka koi evidence nahi.

Par usi comparison se do asli bug nikle:

**(a) FC attitude 87% frames par stale hai.** `fc_d_roll` (jo derotation me
*actually* use hota hai) flight ke 930 frames me se **87% par exactly 0.0** hai —
yani attitude snapshot ~4 Hz par update ho raha hai jabki flow loop 30 Hz par
chal raha hai. Derotation lumps me lag raha hai: teen frame par kuch nahi, chauthe
par pura jama hua tilt. Isliye `fc_d_roll` ka rms 0.98 °/frame aa raha hai jabki
asli motion 0.42 °/frame hai — 2.4× inflated noise. 110 m par 1° = 13.14 px =
1.9 m, to yeh seedha flow ki per-frame quality kharab karta hai (average me
cancel hota hai, velocity bridge me nahi).

**(b) Camera gyro ka sign ULTA hai.** Flight window me:

| | camera gyro | FC |
|---|---|---|
| total roll change | **−2.83°** | **+0.68°** |
| total pitch change | **+0.87°** | **−2.02°** |
| cumulative roll correlation | | **r = −0.80** |

Sums timing-independent hain, aur dono axes par opposite sign hai. `_to_cam()`
ka mapping (code me khud "a GUESS" likha hai) roll/pitch invert kar raha hai —
wahi 180° IMU-vs-camera baat jo HANDOFF §6 me likhi hai. **Agar derotation ko
camera gyro par switch karen (jo (a) ki wajah se karna chahiye), pehle sign
flip karna padega — warna derotation error ko halka karne ke bajaye double kar
dega.**

## 4. "Apne axis par roll/pitch kar raha tha" — naap liya

Hover 106–135 s, ATT @ 10 Hz:

| axis | actual std | p2p | desired std | error std | dominant freq |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roll | **2.40°** | **13.9°** | 1.37° | 2.03° | **0.90 Hz** |
| Pitch | 1.16° | 5.8° | 1.14° | 1.07° | 0.90 Hz (chhota) |

- Demand me 0.90 Hz nahi hai (desired ka main content 0.17 Hz par hai), actual
  aur error dono me hai → **yeh pilot ya position controller nahi, attitude/rate
  loop ka limit cycle hai.**
- Roll pitch se 2× kharab hai → ek taraf ki baat hai, symmetric tuning issue nahi.
- Yaw tracking kamzor: desired yaw rate std 10.6 °/s, achieved 3.5 °/s, error 11.2.
- Rate loop me I-term hi kaam kar raha hai: `PIDR` I mean 0.042 vs P std 0.017 —
  yani ek steady trim offset hai jise I bhar raha hai.

**Hover motor PWM:** M1 1592, M2 1561, M3 1636, M4 1511, M5 1540, M6 1611.
Spread **125 µs**. Left side average 1603 vs right 1548 — **~55 µs (≈5% thrust)
ka lateral imbalance**. `MOTB.FailFlags=2` = thrust_balanced, thrust_boost off —
to ArduPilot ko motor failure nahi lag raha; yeh CoG / prop / arm ki baat hai.
Yahi 47 vs 57 Hz RPM split ko bhi explain karta hai.

Mera suggestion ka order (koi change kiya nahi hai, aapke kehne par karunga):
1. Mechanical pehle — CoG lateral, prop/motor balance, arm clamps, motor mount twist.
2. Harmonic notch theek karo: `INS_HNTCH_FREQ≈47`, `INS_HNTCH_REF=0.33`,
   `INS_HNTCH_BW≈24`, `HMNCS=3` (47 + 94 Hz). Abhi jo 149 Hz par hai wo kuch
   nahi kar raha.
3. Uske baad PID — `ATC_RAT_RLL_P=0.07 / I=0.135 / D=0.0018` aur
   `ATC_RAT_PIT_P=0.09` is 850-class hexa ke liye stock quad values hain.
   0.9 Hz ka wallow under-tuned frame ka classic signature hai. Autotune
   (`AUTOTUNE_AXES=7`, `AGGR=0.075`) already configured hai.

## 7. Aur jo logs me dikha

- **Battery failsafe flight me fire hua** (117.9 s, `ERR Subsys=6 ECode=1`):
  22.57 V, `BATT_LOW_VOLT=22.8`, `BATT_LOW_TIMER=10`. `BATT_FS_LOW_ACT=0` tha
  isliye sirf warning — mode nahi badla. Hover par pack 24.28 → 22.63 V sag
  karta hai, to is threshold par **har flight me failsafe aayega**. 6S ke liye
  22.8 V (3.80 V/cell under load) bahut high threshold hai.
- **Current sensor galat hai:** motors idle par (1150 µs, udna nahi) `BAT.Curr`
  **17.2 A** dikha raha hai, hover par 18.6 A. ZD850 hexa hover ~35–45 A hona
  chahiye. `BATT_AMP_PERVLT=24.0`, `BATT_AMP_OFFSET=0` — offset/scale calibrate
  nahi hai. Matlab mAh counting aur capacity-based failsafe meaningless hain
  (voltage sag asli hai, current reading nahi).
- `MOT_THST_HOVER = 0.20` par asli hover throttle **0.331**. `MOT_HOVER_LEARN=2`
  hai to seekhega, par abhi stale hai — aur throttle-referenced notch isi domain
  me kaam karta hai.
- `ARMING_SKIPCHK = 6156` → compass (4), GPS lock (8), hardware safety switch
  (2048), GPS config (4096) ke pre-arm checks **skip** ho rahe hain.
- Log 215 me do baar `PreArm: Compass calibration running` — background me
  compass calibration chal rahi thi.
- Log 216 me `EKF3 IMU0/IMU1 MAG0 in-flight yaw alignment complete` (105 s) aur
  ek EKF yaw reset — takeoff par compass yaw galat tha, EKF ne hawa me theek kiya.
- GPS/EKF health badhiya: 27 satellites, HDop 0.57, saare innovation ratios
  (SV/SP/SH/SM) 0.00–0.18. Position estimate par shaq ki koi wajah nahi.
- **Koi `RCx_OPTION` set nahi hai** (6–16 sab 0). Isliye Motor Emergency Stop
  jaisa koi switch nahi tha, aur battery nikalna hi ek rasta bacha.

## 9. Mera suggestion (kuch bhi change nahi kiya hai)

Priority order, aapki permission ke baad:

1. **Lidar zero**: field par, zameen par rakhke `RNGFND1_OFFSET` adjust karo
   taaki reading ~0.2–0.3 m aaye. Ab se **har session** me check — yeh ek
   preflight item hai, `verify_before_flight.py` me daal sakte hain.
   `RNGFND1_RMETRIC` bhi 0 hona chahiye (current loop ratiometric nahi hai).
2. **Landing LOITER me mat karo** jab tak lidar theek na ho. Position controller
   ka windup zameen par 13.9° lean aur 1491 µs motors tak gaya — props ke saath
   yeh flip kara sakta hai.
3. **Emergency stop ka rasta** — yeh ek guard hai, isliye pehle pooch raha hoon:
   kisi spare channel par `RCx_OPTION = 31` (Motor Emergency Stop) daalna hai?
   Isse battery nikalne ki zaroorat nahi padegi. Aap bolo to karunga, warna nahi.
4. **Harmonic notch** 47 Hz par shift (§3), uske baad hi PID/autotune soche.
5. **Battery**: `BATT_LOW_VOLT` 6S ke liye realistic karo, aur current sensor
   calibrate karo (`BATT_AMP_PERVLT` / offset) — abhi 17 A idle par dikha raha hai.
6. **SCR_USER har take-off point par phir se type** — aaj 150 m ka offset tha.
7. **Agli flight `--save-frames 2` ke saath**, 80 m se upar, sheet-covered site
   par. Tab tak map-match accuracy ka koi asli number nahi hai.
8. **CSV ko power-safe karo** — aaj do CSV ke tail NUL ho gaye. Periodic
   `flush() + os.fsync()` (har N rows) se flight record power cut me bach jayega.
