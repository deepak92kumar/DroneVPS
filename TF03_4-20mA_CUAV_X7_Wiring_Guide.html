Complete Hardware Integration Manual, Bill of Materials, Wiring Diagram & ArduPilot Setup
Autopilot: CUAV X7 Pro / X7+ Firmware: ArduCopter 4.7.1 Sensor: TF03-4-20mA (5-24V) FC Port: Port 09: ADC (Analog)
1. UART Par '0' Kyun Aa Raha Tha? (Root Cause Explanation)
Aapke LiDAR sensor ke sticker par likha hai: 'TF03-4-20mA 5-24V'.
Yeh standard drone LiDAR (UART) nahi hai, balki factory cranes aur industrial automation ke liye banaya gaya 4-20mA Current-Loop Sensor hai.
Official Benewake TF03 4-20mA User Manual (Page 11 & 14) ke mutabiq:
"Normally, the UART interface can only be used to configure TF03. DO NOT use UART interface to transmit detection data."
Nateeja: Is sensor ka distance UART port par bilkul nahi nikalta (isliye Mission Planner mein 0 aa raha tha). Distance reading sirf Green Wire (Pin 3) par 4mA se 20mA analog current ke roop mein nikalta hai.

5. Resistor Ka Mathematical Formula (Ohm's Law Calculation)
Formula: Voltage (V) = Current (I) × Resistance (R)
Jab hum TF03 ke Green wire aur Ground ke beech 150 Ω resistor lagate hain:
• Minimum Range (0.0 Meter, 4mA current): 0.004 A × 150 Ω = 0.60 Volts
• Maximum Range (180.0 Meter, 20mA current): 0.020 A × 150 Ω = 3.00 Volts
• Voltage Span (ΔV): 3.00 V - 0.60 V = 2.40 Volts
• ArduPilot Scaling Factor: 180.0 m / 2.40 V = 75.0 meters/Volt
• ArduPilot Offset Factor: -(0.60 V × 75.0) = -45.0 meters
Suraksha (Safety): CUAV X7 Pro ka ADC port 0V se 3.3V tak voltage padhta hai. Hamara maximum voltage 3.00V hai, jo flight controller ke safe range ke andar 100% surakshit hai.
6. Step-by-Step Connection Guide (Kaise Jodna Hai)
Step 1: 150-Ohm Resistor Lagana
Resistor ke do siray (leads) hote hain:
1. Pehla sira: TF03 ke GREEN taar aur CUAV JST-GH cable ke Pin 2 (ADC) se jodein.
2. Doosra sira: TF03 ke BLACK taar aur CUAV JST-GH cable ke Pin 4 (GND) se jodein.
(Jod par heat shrink sleeve ya tape chadhakar insulate kar dein taaki aapas mein touch na ho).
Step 2: Power (5V) Connection
TF03 ka RED taar CUAV JST-GH cable ke Pin 1 (+5V) se jodein.
(Agar drone par alag 12V BEC hai, toh Red ko BEC 12V se aur Black ko common ground se jod sakte hain).
Step 3: Faltu Wires Ko Tape Karein
TF03 ke BLUE (Rx), BROWN (Tx) aur WHITE taaron par electrical tape laga dein taaki koi accidental short-circuit na ho.
Step 4: Flight Controller Mein Plug Karein
JST-GH 5-pin connector ko CUAV X7 Pro ke Port 09: ADC socket mein laga dein.

8. Testing & Live Verification (Kaise Check Karein)
1. Connect Karein: Battery lagakar Mission Planner se USB / Telemetry connect karein.
2. Status Tab: FLIGHT DATA screen par neeche Status tab kholiye.
3. sonarvoltage Check: Sensor ko zameen par point karne par lagbhag 0.61V se 0.65V aana chahiye (0 nahi aana chahiye). Agar 0.62V aa raha hai toh circuit 100% theek hai.
4. sonarrange Check: Yeh live distance meters mein dikhayega (jaise 0.15m ya 0.50m).
5. Live Hand Test: TF03 lens ke aage 50 cm par haath rakhein → sonarrange 0.50m dikhayega. Haath door karein → reading smooth badhegi.
6. Pre-Arm Safety: Saare pre-arm checks pass hone chahiye aur 'Rangefinder 1: No Data' error gayab ho jayega.
9. Turant Udaan Ke Liye (Instant Flight Option Without Resistor):
Jab tak aap resistor ya module arrange kar rahe hain, aapka drone abhi bhi bilkul safely udd sakta hai!
