Autonomous Drone Precision Landing Pad

Standardized Optical Target • Dictionary: DICT_4X4_50 • Marker ID: #0

Print & Setup Instructions:
  1. Click Print Landing Pad below (or press Ctrl+P). Set scale to Fit to Printable Area (100%).
  2. For field durability, paste the printout on cardboard, sunboard, or a vinyl mat.
  3. Place this pad at your Takeoff / Home location with the NORTH / FRONT arrow aligned toward your takeoff heading.
  4. The OAK-D Pro downward camera will detect this marker up to 5–10 meters above ground and land directly on the center!
